# Meowmentum 喵動力

Meowmentum is a tiny pixel cat that lives on your macOS desktop and keeps your momentum going. It wanders around, follows your cursor, hops onto your windows, keeps you company while you focus, and takes a nap when you step away.

## Features

### Companion

- **Six modes.** The cat can stay put, wander, follow your cursor, walk on top of other apps' windows, stroll along the Dock, or sleep.

  ![Six modes](docs/screenshots/modes-en.gif)

- **Walk on windows.** The cat jumps onto window title bars and strolls along them. It rides along when you drag a window, and it falls down when the window closes.

  ![Walking on windows](docs/screenshots/windows.gif)

- **Little habits.** While it waits, the cat sits and swishes its tail, washes its face, yawns, stretches, or chases its own tail. When it naps, it curls up, tucks its paws into a loaf, or sleeps belly-up.

  ![Little habits](docs/screenshots/actions-en.gif)

- **Naps.** After a few idle minutes, the cat curls up and sleeps. It wakes up as soon as you come back. Choose the Sleep mode if you want the cat to keep sleeping. In Sleep mode, clicking the cat gives it a pat without waking it up.

  ![Naps](docs/screenshots/naps.gif)

- **Petting.** Move the cursor back and forth over the cat, and it purrs and shows hearts. Poke it five times in a row, and it gets grumpy and walks away.

  ![Petting](docs/screenshots/petting-en.gif)

- **Affection.** Pet, feed, and play with a cat, and you grow closer. Its right-click menu shows your affection as five hearts. A cat that loves you a lot sometimes walks over by itself and rubs against your cursor.

  ![Affection](docs/screenshots/affection-en.gif)

- **Treats with five flavors.** Drop a can from the top of the screen, and the cat runs over to eat it. Each flavor gets a different reaction. Tuna makes the cat happy, chicken makes it purr, salmon makes it jump for joy, catnip gives it the zoomies until it gets dizzy, and bitter melon makes it pull a face and walk away.

  ![Five flavors](docs/screenshots/flavors-en.gif)

- **Toys.** Take out a feather wand or a yarn ball, and every cat chases it and pounces.

  ![Toys](docs/screenshots/toys.gif)

- **Cats play together.** With two or more cats, they chase each other, sniff each other, and groom each other. When it is time to nap, they squeeze together and sleep side by side.

  ![Cats playing together](docs/screenshots/friends-en.gif)

- **Names.** Give each cat a name. Right-click a cat to see its name in its menu.

  ![Names](docs/screenshots/names-en.gif)

- **A little gift.** If you are away for more than 20 minutes, your cat leaves a small gift next to it. Click the gift to collect it.

  ![A little gift](docs/screenshots/gift-en.gif)

### Cat house

- **Home sweet home.** Send one cat or every cat home. The cat walks to the door and goes in, then naps or peeks out of the door and windows. Smoke always curls up from the chimney, and flower bushes by the door drift little petals now and then. When nobody is home, the door and windows stay closed. The house comes in small, medium, and large, and you can drag it anywhere.

  ![Cat house](docs/screenshots/house.gif)

- **Look inside.** Double-click the house to see it as a cutaway dollhouse, right where the house stands on your desktop. The cats walk in through the door and live inside: they nap on the cushion, eat from the bowl, sit by the window, and wander around. Each cat keeps the size you gave it, and you can still pick them up and play with them. Right-click inside the house for the house menu. The inside view has its own size setting, up to huge, so you can watch the room up close. Double-click again to go back outside.

  ![Inside the cat house](docs/screenshots/inside.gif)

- **Pick up and drop.** Drag a cat onto the house and let go, and it goes home. While you look inside, drop a cat in the room and it stays there. Drag it out of the room, and it goes back to your desktop.

  ![Pick up and drop](docs/screenshots/drag-en.gif)

- **The attic.** The house has an attic, open from the start. Right-click the house and choose Go up to the attic. Under the sloped roof there is a big bed, a round window, string lights, and the chimney. Cats curl up on the bed, nap belly-up, warm themselves at the kotatsu or the fireplace, and play with the toy box.

  ![The attic](docs/screenshots/attic-en.gif)

- **Cozy together.** Cats inside the house groom each other, then squeeze together on the cushion and fall asleep.

  ![Cozy together](docs/screenshots/cozy-en.gif)

- **Furniture shop.** Furniture has its own shop, separate from the wardrobe. Right-click the house to open it. Buy a rug, a plant, a lamp, a painting, a scratching post, or a fish tank with swimming fish for the room, a cardboard box or a cat tree for your desktop, and a bookshelf, a kotatsu, or a fireplace for the attic. What you buy appears right away.

  ![Furniture shop](docs/screenshots/furnshop-en.gif)

- **Morning, day, dusk, and night.** The house follows the time of day. In the morning, the sky in the window is peach and the sun is rising. During the day, it is blue with clouds. At dusk, the sunset turns the room orange and the lamps come on. At night, the closed windows turn dark and the lamps glow warmly inside, and a shooting star crosses the window now and then. Late at night, your cat yawns and reminds you to go to bed.

  ![Morning, day, dusk, and night](docs/screenshots/daynight-en.gif)

- **Weather.** Turn on Show the weather in Settings, and the house shows the weather where you are. Rain falls and drips from the eaves, snow drifts down, fog rolls by, and thunderstorms flash. Inside, you see the same weather through the windows, and the glass fogs up. When it starts to rain, a cat may hurry home to stay dry. Meowmentum looks up your approximate city from your IP address once a day and asks [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) for the weather every 30 minutes. No account is needed, and nothing personal is sent.

  ![Weather](docs/screenshots/weather-en.gif)

- **Four seasons and holidays.** The garden changes with the seasons. Spring brings a cherry tree in bloom, summer brings sunflowers and a wind chime, autumn turns the tree red and drops maple leaves into piles, and winter brings a bare tree, a snowman, and snow on the roof. The house also decorates itself for Halloween, Christmas, and Lunar New Year. You can turn this off in Settings.

  ![Seasons and holidays](docs/screenshots/seasons-en.gif)

### Your cats, your style

- **Cat palette.** Design your own cat with a live preview. Pick any fur color, and give it odd eyes with a different color for each eye if you like. Start from any cat, including the built-in colors.

  ![Cat palette](docs/screenshots/palette-en.gif)

- **Mix and match patterns.** Choose a base of solid, calico, or spots, then add tabby stripes, tuxedo white, or both on top. Add details too: Siamese-style points, a darker back that fades into the belly, and their own colors for the ears and the tail. Each layer has its own color, so you can make a striped calico, a tabby with white paws, or anything else. Give each color its own name, or leave it blank and it is numbered automatically.

  ![Coat patterns](docs/screenshots/patterns-en.gif)

- **Accessories.** Dress your cats in a party hat, a crown, a beanie, a flower clip, a bell collar, a bow tie, or a scarf from the wardrobe. A witch hat is free around Halloween, and a Santa hat is free in December. Some accessories, such as a star clip, a halo, a medal, and a bandana, are rewards for badges.

  ![Accessories](docs/screenshots/accessories.gif)

- **Desktop furniture.** Put a cardboard box or a cat tree on your desktop. Cats wander over by themselves. In the box, only their head peeks out. On the cat tree, they sit on the top platform.

  ![Desktop furniture](docs/screenshots/furniture.gif)

- **More cats.** Adopt several cats. Each one remembers its own color, accessories, name, affection, and settings.

  ![More cats](docs/screenshots/cats.gif)

### Productivity

- **Pomodoro timer.** Focus for 15, 25, or 50 minutes, or set your own time. The cat you start the timer from is the one that keeps you company. A pixel tomato above the cat ripens as time passes. It starts yellow-green, turns yellow-orange halfway, and becomes red when time is up. Then the cat walks to the middle of the screen to remind you, and a break starts automatically.

  ![Pomodoro timer](docs/screenshots/pomodoro-en.gif)

- **Focus sounds.** Pick a soft purr, gentle rain, or a crackling fireplace in Settings. It plays quietly while you focus and stops for your break. The sounds are generated by the app, so there is nothing extra to download.

  ![Focus sounds](docs/screenshots/sounds-en.gif)

- **Meeting reminders.** Turn on Meeting reminders in Settings and allow access to Calendar. Five minutes before a meeting, a cat runs to the middle of the screen to tell you, and the other cats head home. Your calendar is only read on your Mac.

  ![Meeting reminders](docs/screenshots/meetings-en.gif)

- **Earn fish by focusing.** Every finished focus session earns dried fish, one for every 5 minutes. Spend them on accessories in the wardrobe and on furniture in the furniture shop.

  ![Fish shop](docs/screenshots/fish-en.gif)

- **Focus stats.** See today's focus time, your streak, and your total hours, with a pixel calendar of the last 16 weeks.

  ![Focus stats](docs/screenshots/stats-en.gif)

- **Badges and a cat diary.** Earn badges for your first focus, 3-day and 7-day streaks, 10 hours of focus, a 90-minute session, trying every flavor, adopting three cats, and a best friend with full affection. Some badges unlock accessories. The stats window also keeps a little diary of each day: how long you focused, how many treats the cats ate, how often they were petted, and more.

  ![Badges and diary](docs/screenshots/badges-en.gif)

- **Quiet focus.** Turn on "Cats go home while I focus" in Settings. The other cats go home to nap, and one cat stays to show the countdown. They come back out for your break.

  ![Quiet focus](docs/screenshots/quiet-en.gif)

- **Custom focus and break times.** Pick any length from 1 to 180 minutes with the plus and minus buttons, the arrow keys, or the quick presets. Your recent custom times appear in the picker, and you can remove any of them.

  ![Custom focus time](docs/screenshots/custom-time-en.gif)

- **To-do list.** Open a game-style quest log from the right-click menu or the menu bar. When every quest is checked, the cat spins with joy.

  ![To-do list](docs/screenshots/todo-en.gif)

- **Break reminders.** Every 30, 45, or 60 minutes, the cat reminds you to stretch, drink water, or rest your eyes. It stays quiet while you focus or when you are away.

  ![Break reminders](docs/screenshots/reminders-en.gif)

- **Low battery alerts.** Below 20% battery, the cat gets sleepy and asks you to charge. When you plug in, it thanks you.

  ![Low battery alerts](docs/screenshots/battery-en.gif)

## Battery-friendly by design

| Area | How it saves power |
| --- | --- |
| Windows | Each cat is a window exactly the size of the cat, so moving it costs almost nothing. |
| Timers | Timers run only when needed. A walking cat moves at 60 fps with time-based speed, and cats rest between walks. |
| Background checks | One light check every 0.4 seconds detects idle time and petting. |
| Sleeping | A sleeping cat only breathes once per second. |
| Images | Sprites are cut, scaled, and cached once at startup. |
| Battery | The battery is read once every 2 minutes. |
| Cat house | The house animates only its own small window, with coalesced timers. When the house is hidden, it runs no timers at all. The inside view is drawn in the same small window and animates only while you are looking at it. |
| Toys and furniture | A toy follows the cursor only while it is out, and it is put away after 90 seconds. Furniture runs no timers. Cats check for furniture and friends only every 14 to 25 seconds. |
| Weather and effects | Weather is off until you turn it on, and then it is checked once every 30 minutes. Rain, snow, fish, the fire, and shooting stars animate on one shared timer, and only while there is something to draw. |
| Focus sounds | Sounds play only during focus, with the built-in `afplay`. Each sound is generated once and reused. |
| Meeting reminders | The calendar is checked once a minute, only when the reminders are turned on. |
| Low Power Mode | In Low Power Mode, the house's petal and weather effects run less often. |
| Dock mode | The Dock size is read from the Dock settings at most once every 20 seconds, and only in "Walk on the Dock" mode. |
| Window detection | Window positions are read only in "Walk on windows" mode. Window contents are never read, so no screen-recording permission is needed. |

## Install

### Option 1: Download the app (easiest)

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/Tanuki3701/meowmentum/releases/latest).
2. Download `Meowmentum-apple-silicon.zip` for M-series Macs, or `Meowmentum-intel.zip` for Intel Macs. To check, open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac.
3. Unzip it and drag **Meowmentum** into your Applications folder.
4. Open it once. Because the app is not signed with a paid Apple developer account, macOS stops it the first time. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway**. You only need to do this once.

You need macOS 11 Big Sur or later. No Python is needed.

### Option 2: Build the app yourself

Building it on your own Mac avoids the security warning.

You need macOS 11 Big Sur or later, plus Python 3.9 or later and Git. If you do not have them, run `xcode-select --install` in Terminal. It installs both. The first build needs an internet connection to download the dependencies.

Open Terminal and run these three lines. The first build takes a few minutes.

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Tanuki3701/meowmentum.git
cd meowmentum
bash build_mac.sh
```

If you downloaded the ZIP from GitHub instead, unzip it, then run `bash build_mac.sh` from that folder in Terminal. Run it with `bash` rather than double-clicking, because macOS blocks double-clicked scripts that came from the internet.

The app is installed to your Applications folder. No administrator password is needed. If the Applications folder is not writable, the app goes to `~/Applications` instead.

### Option 3: Run from source

Run `bash run.command` in Terminal. The first run installs the dependencies automatically.

You can also run it manually.

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt
.venv/bin/python src/main.py
```

### Open at login

Right-click a cat, open Settings, and choose **Open at login**.

## Usage

- **Right-click** the cat (or two-finger click on a trackpad) to open the menu for that cat.
- **Click the cat icon in the menu bar** to control all cats at once.
- **Double-click the cat house** to look inside. **Right-click the house** to go up to the attic or open the furniture shop.
- **Drag a cat onto the house** to send it home.
- Press **Control-Option-M** to hide or show all cats.
- Every feature is in the right-click menu. A left click just gives the cat a pat, and it wakes up a sleeping cat.
- **Drag** the cat with the left mouse button to move it.
- To quit, right-click the cat and choose **Quit**.

```
│   ├── version.py       # Version number
│   └── macos.py         # macOS helpers: Spaces, idle time, windows, glass, shortcut, login item
├── assets/
│   ├── sprites/         # Cat sprites (walking, sleeping, expressions, faces), effects, cat house
│   ├── fonts/           # Fusion Pixel font (SIL OFL 1.1)
│   ├── icon.png         # App icon
│   └── tray.png         # Menu bar icon
├── tools/
│   ├── make_sprites.py      # Generates sprites, expressions, the cat house, and the icons

├── build_mac.sh         # One-step build and install
└── run.command          # Run from source
```

### 安裝

#### 方法一：直接下載 App (最簡單)

1. 打開[最新版本](https://github.com/Tanuki3701/meowmentum/releases/latest)的頁面。
2. M 系列晶片的 Mac 請下載 `Meowmentum-apple-silicon.zip`，Intel 的 Mac 請下載 `Meowmentum-intel.zip`，不確定的話可以在蘋果選單的「關於這台 Mac」查看。
3. 解壓縮後把 **Meowmentum** 拖到「應用程式」資料夾。
4. 打開一次，因為這個 App 沒有用付費的 Apple 開發者帳號簽署，第一次打開時 macOS 會擋下來，請到「系統設定」的「隱私權與安全性」往下捲動後按「**強制打開**」，只需要做一次。

需要 macOS 11 Big Sur 以上，不需要安裝 Python。

#### 方法二：自己打包

在自己的 Mac 上打包就不會跳出安全性警告。

需要 macOS 11 Big Sur 以上，以及 Python 3.9 以上和 Git，如果還沒有的話請在終端機執行 `xcode-select --install`，兩個都會一起裝好，第一次打包需要網路來下載套件。

打開終端機執行下面三行，第一次打包需要幾分鐘。

```bash
git clone https://github.com/Tanuki3701/meowmentum.git
cd meowmentum
bash build_mac.sh
```

如果你是從 GitHub 下載 ZIP，請先解壓縮再在終端機進到那個資料夾執行 `bash build_mac.sh`，請用 `bash` 執行而不要直接雙擊，因為 macOS 會擋下從網路下載後直接雙擊開啟的腳本。

完成後 App 會安裝到「應用程式」資料夾，不需要管理者密碼，如果「應用程式」資料夾不能寫入，App 會改裝到 `~/Applications`。

#### 開機自動打開

在貓咪身上按右鍵，打開「設定」並選「**開機時自動打開**」。

### 使用方式

- 在貓咪身上**按右鍵** (觸控板雙指點按) 可以打開那隻貓的選單。
- **點選單列上的小貓圖示**可以一次控制全部的貓。
- 在**貓屋上按兩下**可以進去看看，在**貓屋上按右鍵**可以上閣樓或打開家具店。
- **把貓咪拖到貓屋上**放開就會回家。
- 按 **Control-Option-M** 可以藏起或叫回所有貓咪。
- 所有功能都在右鍵選單裡，用左鍵點貓咪只會摸摸頭，點睡著的貓咪會把牠叫醒。
- 用滑鼠左鍵**拖曳**可以移動貓咪。
- 要結束程式時，在貓咪身上按右鍵並選擇**結束**。
